01 / THE IDEA

A familiar gesture.
A different kind of money.

RGB cash bill explores physical digital dollars: a bill shows an amount, another bill receives it, and the docks manage the wallets behind the scenes. The goal is a transfer that feels tangible, without a QR code or a phone app at the tap.

↔

Local exchange

The invoice and exact consignment travel through Bluetooth between the bills.

◎

Independent custody

Each bill has its own wallet, opened by its dock. The ESP32 handles the display and radio.

✓

Intent before action

A press arms discovery. An exact, one-use confirmation authorizes the spend on the dock.
